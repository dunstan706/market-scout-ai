# Market Scout AI

Idea: An AI-powered intelligence platform that continuously monitors what is happening around a local business and tells the owner what changed, why it matters, and what they should do.

What it monitors

Competitor prices, services, promotions, and hours

Google/business reviews and sentiment

New competitors opening nearby

Competitors closing or expanding

Local business websites and social activity

New developments, offices, apartments, and other local changes

Market pricing and customer-demand patterns

What the user gets

Instead of a giant dashboard, they get something like:

Weekly Market Brief

🔴 Competitor A dropped prices 15%
🟡 New salon opening 700m away
🟢 Customers increasingly praise your fast service

Recommendation: Test a weekday promotion and emphasize fast service in your marketing.

Initial target

Start with one vertical—e.g. salons, gyms, restaurants, or real-estate agents—rather than every business.

Business model

Charge roughly ₹1,000–₹7,500+/month, depending on how much intelligence and automation you provide.

Why it's interesting

Your OSINT technology becomes the underlying engine, but instead of selling OSINT, you're selling a business outcome:

"We'll watch your local market for you and tell you what you need to know."

The long-term vision is AI market research for small businesses—including eventually recommending where someone should open their next location.
